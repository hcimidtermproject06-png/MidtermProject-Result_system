Survey Instruments

Heuristic Evaluation Workbook

Each group member performed individual heuristic evaluations using a workbook based on usability principles such as consistency, error prevention, and efficiency.

IV. Severity Rating Scale

0 – No problem

1 – Cosmetic issue only

2 – Minor usability problem

3 – Major usability problem

4 – Critical usability problem

Usability Testing Survey and Instrument used

Instrument Used: Questionnaire (Usability Testing Survey)

The system evaluation used the questionnaire as the main instrument. the questionnaire was design as a usability testing survey to avaluate the ease of use, efficiency, and user satisfaction of the system.

It includes items based on theSystem Usability Scale (SUS) and other usability- related questions.
