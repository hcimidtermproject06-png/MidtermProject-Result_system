Survey Results and Analysis

Online Transparency System for Barangay Telegrapo

Narrative Report Presentation

Overall Summary:

A total of 25 respondents participated in the survey evaluation of the system. Most respondents were female students aged 21 years old and are currently enrolled in the BSENT program. The majority of respondents use technology daily and already have experience using similar systems.

The questionnaire results show that respondents generally rated the system positively, particularly in usability, accessibility, and user confidence. Most responses fall under ratings 4 (Agree) and 5 (Strongly Agree), indicating that users are satisfied with the overall performance of the system.

Note: Findings are based on 25 respondents only and may still improve through further testing and evaluation.

Interpretation of Results
Findings:

The survey results indicate that the respondents generally evaluated the system positively. Most participants selected ratings of 4 (Agree) and 5 (Strongly Agree) across the questionnaire items.

Questions 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 received high agreement ratings, showing that users found the system useful, understandable, and reliable. Meanwhile, Questions 4, 6, and 8 had more neutral responses, suggesting that some parts of the system may still need improvement in terms of design, navigation, or functionality.
Conclusion:

Based on the gathered data, the Online Transparency System for Barangay Telegrapo is considered functional, user-friendly, and acceptable to users. Respondents were able to use the system effectively and showed confidence in its features.

However, further enhancement of the interface, navigation flow, and feature integration is recommended to improve the overall user experience and increase user satisfaction.
Recommendations
1. Improve User Interface:
Enhance the layout, design consistency, and readability of the system.
2. Enhance System Navigation:
Provide clearer labels, menus, and instructions for easier navigation.
3. Conduct Further Testing:
Increase the number of respondents for more accurate and reliable results.
4. Add More Features:
Include additional functionalities that can further improve transparency and accessibility.
